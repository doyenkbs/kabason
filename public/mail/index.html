Mail apps on your phone or computer cannot use your normal sign-in password. Instead, create an app password on the app passwords page and type that into the mail app wherever it asks for a password.

Mail app settings

If your mail app asks for server details, use these.

SettingValue
Incoming (IMAP) servermail.kabason.com
IMAP port / security993 / SSL/TLS
Outgoing (SMTP) servermail.kabason.com
SMTP port / security465 / SSL/TLS or 587 / STARTTLS if the app only offers that
Usernameyour full email address
Passwordan app password from mail.kabason.com/account

Thunderbird fills these in automatically from your email address.

Step by step

Before you start, create an app password on the app passwords page. Keep it open, you will type it in below.

iPhone or iPad (Mail app)
  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Apps.
  3. Tap Mail.
  4. Tap Mail Accounts.
  5. Tap Add Account.
  6. Type your full email address, then tap Next.
  7. Tap Add Other Account.
  8. Tap Mail Account.
  9. Fill in your name, your email address, your app password, and a description such as "Kabason".
  10. Tap Next.
  11. If Mail finds your settings, tap Done. You are finished.
  12. If it does not, tap IMAP.
  13. Fill in the incoming and outgoing mail server details from the settings table, then tap Next.
  14. Tap Save.

These steps match current iPhone and iPad software. On an older version, some menu names may be different.

Android (Gmail app)
  1. Open the Gmail app.
  2. Tap your profile picture at the top right.
  3. Tap Add another account.
  4. Tap Other.
  5. Type your full email address, then tap Next.
  6. Tap Personal (IMAP).
  7. Type your app password, then tap Next.
  8. When it asks for server settings, use the settings table.
  9. Follow the remaining screens to finish.
New Outlook for Windows
  1. Open Outlook.
  2. Select the View tab, then View settings.
  3. Select Accounts, then Your accounts.
  4. Under Email accounts, select Add Account.
  5. Type your full email address, then select Continue.
  6. When it asks for a password, type your app password, then select Done.

If Outlook asks for server details along the way, use the settings table.

Classic Outlook for Windows
  1. Open Outlook.
  2. Select File, then Add Account.
  3. Type your full email address.
  4. Select Advanced options.
  5. Check Let me set up my account manually.
  6. Select Connect.
  7. Select IMAP.
  8. Fill in the incoming and outgoing server details from the settings table, then select Next.
  9. Type your app password, then select Connect.
Outlook for Mac
  1. Open Outlook.
  2. In the menu bar at the top of the screen, select Outlook, then Settings.
  3. Select Accounts.
  4. Select + Add Account.
  5. Type your full email address, then select Continue.
  6. Follow the prompts. Use your app password when it asks for a password, and the settings table if it asks for server details.
  7. Select Done.

On the older Outlook for Mac, step 3 is Accounts under Personal Settings, and step 4 is Add Email Account.

Thunderbird
  1. Open Thunderbird. The first time you open it, the account setup screen appears by itself. If it does, skip to step 4.
  2. Select the ≡ menu button.
  3. Select New Account, then Existing Email.
  4. Fill in your name, your full email address, and your app password.
  5. Select Continue.
  6. Thunderbird finds the server settings by itself. Select Done.
